# Workspace turns purple at midnight

In this fictional CloudNest support scenario, a scheduled theme rule can change
the workspace to purple at 00:00 in the device's local time zone.

1. Open Settings > Appearance > Theme schedule.
2. Check for an enabled Purple or Violet theme rule starting at 00:00 (midnight), and confirm your device's time zone.
3. If that rule exists, disable or remove it; turn off Scheduled themes if you do not want automatic theme changes.
4. Select your preferred Light, Dark, or System theme, click Apply, and reload the workspace.
5. If there is no matching rule or the problem returns, contact support with your client version, local time, and time zone.
